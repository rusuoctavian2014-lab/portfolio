Hi, I'm Octavian.

I like to notice things other people walk past.

I'm a photographer and visual creator from Romania, drawn to moments that are easy to overlook — a bird caught in the right light, a landscape from above, a quiet street or a frame that simply feels right.

Photography started as a way of observing the world differently. Over time, it became a way of telling stories through light, composition and perspective.

I work across photography, aerial imagery and visual design, always looking for the balance between technical precision and something instinctive.
